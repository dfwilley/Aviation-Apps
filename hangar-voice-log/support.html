Hangar Log Help & Support

Hangar Log turns spoken notes and photos into maintenance logbook entries for your airplane.

Contact support: nitty.willey@gmail.com
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Getting started

  1. On first launch, enter your tail number, make and model, and your name. You can change these any time with the gear button.
  2. Add your standard jobs (for example, oil change) with your usual logbook wording. Use [ ] where a number changes each time.
  3. For each job: pick it, check the date and Hobbs, then tap Talk and describe the work, or type it. Add photos if you like.
  4. Tap Share to send the entry to Mail, Messages, Files, or a printer, then copy it into your logbook. Tap Mark as logged when done.

Common questions

Talk doesn't turn my words into text.

Check iPhone Settings › Hangar Log and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are on. The recording is kept with the entry even if text recognition fails.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy. Your entries are also visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › Hangar Log.

What is the Access database link?

An optional feature for owners who keep their logbook in a Microsoft Access database set up to import Hangar Log entries. If you don't use one, skip it and use Share.

Will I lose my entries if I update the app?

No. Updates keep your entries. Deleting the app deletes them, so share or export anything you want to keep first.
