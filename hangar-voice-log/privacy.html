Hangar Log Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Hangar Log is an iPhone app that helps aircraft owners write maintenance logbook entries from spoken notes, typed text, and photos. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

The following are saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • Your aircraft details (tail number, make and model) and your name as you type them in Settings.
  • Logbook entries you write, including dates, Hobbs times, and hours of work.
  • Voice recordings you make in the app.
  • Photos you take or choose for an entry.
  • Standard jobs (your usual logbook wording) you create.

There is no account to create and no server operated by the developer. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap Talk, the app records your voice and uses Apple's speech recognition to turn it into text. On iPhones that support it, recognition is done entirely on the phone. On phones that don't, Apple may process the audio to return the text, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives your recordings or the text.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo to an entry. Only the photos you pick are copied into the app.

Sharing and the optional folder link

Information leaves the app only when you choose to send it:

  • Share: sends the entry text and its photos to the app or person you select (for example Mail, Messages, or Files).
  • Access database link (optional): if you link a folder in Settings, the app reads a data file from that folder and adds finished entries to an import file there. If that folder is in a cloud service such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete any entry, recording, or photo in the app, and clear your details in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf. iPhone backups you make (for example to iCloud) may include the app's data, as described by Apple.

Permissions

You can turn microphone, speech recognition, camera, and photo access on or off at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Hangar Log. The app still works for typed entries with those permissions off.

Children

Hangar Log is intended for aircraft owners and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
