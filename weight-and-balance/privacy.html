Homebuilt W&B Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Homebuilt W&B is a weight and balance calculator for amateur-built aircraft, available as an iPhone app and a web app. In short: everything you enter stays on your device. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

The following are saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone (or, for the web app, in your browser's storage on your device):

  • Your aircraft registration, model and landing gear type.
  • Scale weights, arms, weight limits and CG limits you enter.
  • The loads you enter: people, baggage and fuel.

There is no account to create and no server operated by the developer. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos, and it does not connect to the internet.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you change it or delete the app (or, for the web app, clear the site's data in your browser). Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf. iPhone backups you make (for example to iCloud) may include the app's data, as described by Apple.

Children

Homebuilt W&B is intended for pilots and aircraft owners and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
