Homebuilt W&B Help & Support

Homebuilt W&B is a weight and balance calculator for RV and other amateur-built aircraft.

Contact support: open a question on the app's support board. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and aircraft model.

Getting started

  1. On first launch, enter your registration, pick your model, and choose nosewheel or tailwheel. Picking a model fills in its published CG range, weight limits and arms.
  2. Tap Empty weight ✎ and enter the three scale weights and their arms from your aircraft's weighing.
  3. Tap Limits and arms ✎ and check every number against your aircraft's operating limitations and weight and balance record. Change any that differ.
  4. Enter people, baggage, takeoff fuel and fuel burned. The status line, CG chart and loading table update as you type.

Common questions

Where do the model defaults come from?

From Van's Aircraft's published weight and balance quick-reference sheets, the RV-12 Pilot's Operating Handbook, and Van's specification tables. The "Limits and sources" section at the bottom of the app names the source for your model and says which numbers you should double-check.

My airplane's numbers are different.

That is normal for amateur-built aircraft; the builder sets the gross weight. Tap Limits and arms ✎ and change the weight limits, the forward and aft CG limits for each category, the seat, baggage and fuel tank arms, the baggage limits and the fuel capacity.

How do I get the defaults back?

In Limits and arms, tap Restore default limits and arms. Your scale readings and loads are kept.

I only have a total empty weight and empty CG.

Enter them on one line of the empty weight editor (weight and arm) and leave the other two lines at 0.

My model isn't listed.

Choose Other / custom values and enter the limits and arms from your own records.

How do I change the registration, model, or nosewheel/tailwheel?

Tap Aircraft at the top right.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Important

This app is a planning aid, not an approved source of data. Your aircraft's own weight and balance record and operating limitations govern, and the pilot in command is responsible for loading the aircraft within its limits. Homebuilt W&B is not affiliated with or endorsed by Van's Aircraft, Inc.
